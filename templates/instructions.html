Before We Begin

Thank you for volunteering! We will start with a brief webcam calibration to align the gaze tracker with your eyes. Please read the steps below so the calibration is as accurate as possible.

What to do

  1. Find a well-lit spot, sit at arm's length from the screen, and keep the webcam at eye level.
  2. Remove anything covering your face (e.g., hats) and make sure your eyes are clearly visible.
  3. Click Start Calibration below. Your browser will ask for webcam permission, so please allow it.
  4. You will see nine blue dots arranged around the screen and a small webcam window. For each dot:
    • Look directly at the dot while keeping your head as still as you can.
    • Click the dot once while continuing to stare at it. The dot will turn gray to show it was recorded.
  5. Work through all nine dots (corners, edges, and center). When the last dot turns gray, calibration finishes automatically and the practice session begins.
  6. If something goes wrong, refresh the page to restart the calibration.

Take your time during calibration - the more precise you are now, the easier the study will feel.
